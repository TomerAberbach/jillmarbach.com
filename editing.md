# Editing the website

Go to <https://jillmarbach.com/admin> and sign in with GitHub.

Saving publishes your change. Give the website a few minutes to update, then
refresh the page.

## Posts

- **Add a post:** open **Posts** and create a new one. Choose the title before
  you save, because the title and date set the post's web address.
- **Edit a post:** open it, make your change, and save.
- **Delete a post:** open it and delete it.

Each post has these fields:

- **Title**
- **Publish Date:** posts are sorted by this date. A future date does not delay
  publishing.
- **Tags:** put each tag on its own row. Use the same spelling as your other
  posts.
- **Cover Image:** the picture on the post's card and in links shared on social
  media. To show it in the post too, add it to **Content**.
- **Content:** when you add an image, describe it in its alt text field so
  people using screen readers know what it shows.

## Images and files

- Upload the original image. The editor shrinks photos to 2,000 pixels and
  converts them to WebP. Animated GIFs stay as they are.
- Files can be at most 5 MB. For a larger one, such as a video, upload it
  elsewhere and link to it. Photos are measured after they shrink.
- To link to a PDF or another file, upload it under **Assets** in the main files
  folder. Link to it as `/files/` followed by its name, such as
  `/files/story.pdf`. If you link to a file on Google Drive, share it with
  "Anyone with the link".

## Pages

- **Home:** the introduction and featured posts.
- **Settings:** your name, email address, website description, social media
  profiles, and resume.

## If your change does not appear

Wait ten minutes and refresh the page. If it is still missing, the website
probably could not be rebuilt, and visitors still see the previous version. Your
saved change is safe. Contact Tomer and say what you changed.

Never delete an image that a post uses. The website cannot be rebuilt until the
image is back.
